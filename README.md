# Global Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing Global Superstore data using Microsoft Power BI to understand business performance, product profitability, customer purchasing behaviour, and shipping costs.

## Objectives
- Analyse overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify profitable and loss-making product categories.
- Understand customer segment contributions.
- Analyse regional sales performance.
- Evaluate shipping expenses and identify opportunities for cost optimisation.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
- Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Loss and Orders.
- Regional sales performance.
- Category-level business performance.
- Sales trends over time.

### 2. Product & Category Analysis
- Category-wise profit and loss.
- Sub-category profitability.
- Top-performing products.
- Identification of loss-making areas.

### 3. Customer Purchasing Behaviour & Shipping Analysis
- Customer segment contribution.
- Customer purchasing patterns.
- Shipping cost analysis.
- Average shipping cost per order.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel

## Key Business Insights
- Total Sales: 12.64M
- Total Profit: 1.47M
- Total Loss: 843.84K
- Total Orders: 25K
- Technology is the strongest category by displayed profit.
- Furniture requires further profitability investigation.
- Tables recorded a negative result of approximately 64.1K.
- Consumer segment contributes 56.43% of sales.

## Business Recommendations
- Review pricing and discount strategies for loss-making products.
- Investigate Furniture and Tables profitability.
- Analyse regional sales differences.
- Monitor shipping expenses and identify cost optimisation opportunities.
